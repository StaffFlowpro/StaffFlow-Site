Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

StaffFlow is timesheet-to-invoice automation software for staffing firms. This policy explains what data we handle, where it goes, and who else can see it. It is written to describe what the software actually does, rather than to reserve rights we do not use.

In short. Your timesheets and invoices remain in systems you own — your Microsoft 365 tenant and your QuickBooks company. StaffFlow reads from them and writes back to them. To read a timesheet, its contents pass through StaffFlow’s own self-hosted workflow engine, Microsoft Azure and OpenAI. We do not sell data, and we do not use your data to train models.

Who we are

StaffFlow is operated by Saichandra Bandi, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. For any question about this policy, or to exercise a right described below, write to hello@staffflowpro.com.

Where StaffFlow processes data on behalf of a customer, that customer is the data controller and StaffFlow is a processor acting on their instructions. The terms of that processing are set out in our Data Processing Agreement, which forms part of our Terms of Service.

What data StaffFlow handles

  • Configuration. Consultant names, placement records, bill rates, pay rates and pay percentages, vendor names, and routing email addresses. Entered by the customer.
  • Timesheet content. Documents received in the customer's mailbox, and the hours read from them.
  • Invoice and pay figures. Invoice amounts, periods, and the QuickBooks identifiers of invoices created; and the pay amounts StaffFlow computes from the customer's pay rates and percentages.
  • Operational logs. Errors, processing outcomes, and the identifiers of emails handled — used to investigate failures.
  • Account data. The name, email address, role, password hash and, where enabled, encrypted two-step sign-in secret of each dashboard user.

StaffFlow does not request or store payment card details, bank account numbers, social security numbers, or tax identifiers. Where a customer enters pay rates or pay percentages, StaffFlow uses them only to compute pay figures and margin for the customer's review; it does not run payroll, make payments, or hold the bank details of anyone it pays.

Where your records live

StaffFlow does not become a second system of record. Timesheet documents are filed into the customer's own OneDrive. Invoices are created in the customer's own QuickBooks company. StaffFlow's database holds the configuration, the hours recorded, the invoice references and pay figures, the audit history behind them, and the operational logs described above.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not permit our processors to use customer content to train their models.

QuickBooks data

StaffFlow accesses a customer's QuickBooks company only after an authorised user of that company grants access through Intuit's consent screen. We request the minimum scope needed to read customers, create invoices, and read the status and payments of the invoices StaffFlow created or the customer linked to it. Access can be revoked at any time from within QuickBooks, or by writing to us. StaffFlow does not read bank feeds, payroll, or transactions unrelated to those invoices.

Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted with TLS.
  • QuickBooks access tokens and two-step sign-in secrets are encrypted by StaffFlow before storage. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.
  • Access to the dashboard requires authentication, and every request is scoped to a single customer.
  • StaffFlow staff access customer data only to operate the service and resolve issues, and do not review timesheet contents beyond what an issue requires.
  • Credentials and API tokens are held as environment secrets, never in source code.

The full set of measures is described on our Security page. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and describe what happened.

Retention

Configuration, hours, and invoice records are retained for as long as the customer's account is active, because they are the audit trail behind invoices already issued. Operational logs are retained while they remain useful for investigation. On written request, and where no legal or accounting obligation requires otherwise, we will return or delete a customer's data within thirty days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. Where StaffFlow acts as a processor for a customer, please direct such a request to that customer; we will assist them in answering it. Otherwise, write to hello@staffflowpro.com.

International transfers

StaffFlow is operated from India. Its processors operate infrastructure in the United States and elsewhere. Data handled by StaffFlow may therefore be processed outside the country in which it was collected.

Children

StaffFlow is business software. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and, where the change affects an active customer, tell them directly.
