Where your data lives

Your records stay yours.

Timesheets stay in your OneDrive. Invoices stay in your QuickBooks. StaffFlow reads from your systems and writes back to them — it never becomes a second place your client records live.

We hold what the automation needs: your placement and rate configuration, the hours billed, and error logs. Not your books.

To read a timesheet, its contents pass through Microsoft Azure and OpenAI. We name every processor in the privacy policy, and say plainly what each one sees.
