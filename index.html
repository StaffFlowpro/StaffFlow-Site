Questions

Straight answers.

Does Vantusk send invoices to our clients?

No. It creates the invoice in your QuickBooks. You send it the way you do today, by QuickBooks email or through the client's vendor portal, and Vantusk tracks whether it was sent and paid.

What if the AI misreads a timesheet?

The AI only reads; it never decides what to bill. Vantusk checks what it read against the sheet's own printed totals, and a sheet with no printed total is read twice and the two readings compared. Anything that doesn't add up waits for a person, with the reason.

Can anything be invoiced without us seeing it?

No. No invoice is created without a person approving it, and approval is refused while anything in that period is still waiting for review.

Do we need Microsoft 365?

No. You can upload timesheets on the dashboard today. Reading timesheets straight from your Microsoft 365 mailbox is being switched on. On Google Workspace, email forwarding is set up with you during setup.

Is our data used to train AI?

No. Neither Vantusk nor the services that read documents use your data to train AI models.

How long does setup take?

Most firms are set up in about two working days. Your first invoices go out on your next billing cycle.

What does it cost?

Priced per active consultant per month. We share pricing after a short welcome call, once we've seen how you bill.
